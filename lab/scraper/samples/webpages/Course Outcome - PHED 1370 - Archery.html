Learning Outcome
 
At the end of the course, students are able to
1. demonstrate the basic skills of archery;
2. acquire the basic knowledge of the sport of archery;
3. acquire the knowledge of safety in the sport of archery;
4. describe the basic rules, scoring, terminology and equipment of archery;
5. acquire basic knowledge in competing in archery competition;
6. demonstrate positive attitudes and values toward sports participation.
 
完成課程後，學生能夠
1. 展示射箭基本技術;
2. 掌握基本的射箭常識;
3. 掌握射箭運動的安全知識;
4. 掌握射箭運動的基本規則、計分法、名詞及設備;
5. 掌握射箭比賽的基本知識;
6. 培養對參與運動的正面態度及價值觀。
 
Course Syllabus
 
Teaching Content
1.      History of archery and introduction of archery equipment and maintenance
2.      Introduction of rules and scoring of the games
3.      Setting up the bow and arrow
4.      Shooting procedures & aiming target
5.      Target shooting in 10, 15, 18, 25 and 30 meters
 
教學內容:
1.     射箭用具介紹及其護理方法
2.     射箭比賽規則及計分法之介紹
3.     上弦搭箭的正確方法
4.     射箭之正確姿勢及瞄準方法
5.     10米、18米及25米等距離之射靶練習
